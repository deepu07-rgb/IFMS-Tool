Consequent upon promulgation of the Rajasthan Civil Services (Revised Pay) 
Rules, 2017 with effect from 01.10.2017, the Governor is pleased to order that the 
State Government employees may be allowed Dearness Allowance with Pay in the 
Level in the Pay Matrix from the date mentioned below at the following rate;-
Date from which payable
Rate of Dearness Allowance 
per mensem
From 01.10.2017
5%
2. 
The payment of Dearness Allowance under this order from 01.10.2017 shall be 
made after adjusting the amount of Dearness Allowance already paid from 01.10.2017, 
if any, under the existing orders.
3. 
The term ‘Pay' for the purpose of calculation of Dearness Allowance shall be 
the Basic Pay i.e. pay drawn in the Pay Matrix of the prescribed Levels and shall not 
include any other type(s) of pay like Special Pay or Personal Pay etc,
4. 
The payment on account of Dearness Allowance involving fraction of 50 paisa 
and above may be rounded off to the next higher rupee and the fractions of less than 50 
paisa may be ignored.
