GOVERNMENT OF RAJASTHAN
FINANCE DEPARTMENT
(RULES DIVISION)
No. F. 6(3) FD (Rules)/2017
ORDER
Jaipur, dated:ri 4 OCT 2024
Sub: Grant of Dearness Allowance to State Government employees.
The Governor is pleased to order that the existing rate of Dearness
Allowance payable to the State Government employees, drawing pay in the
Rajasthan Civil Services (Revised Pay) Rules, 2017, under Finance Department
Order of even number dated 14-03-2024 shall be revised from 50% to 53% with
effect from 01-07-2024.
2.
The term 'Pay' for the purpose of calculation of Dearness Allowance shall be
the Basic Pay i.e. pay drawn in the Pay Matrix of the prescribed Levels and
shall not include any other type(s) of pay like Special Payor Personal Pay etc.
3.
The payment on account of Dearness Allowance involving fraction of 50 paisa
and above may be rounded off to the next higher rupee and the fraction of less
than 50 paisa may be ignored.
4.
The amount of increase in Dearness Allowance for the period from 01-07-2024
to 31-10-2024 shall be credited to the GPF Account as per provisions of
Rajasthan Government Servants General Provident Fund Rules, 2021 as
under:
(i)
For the employees recruited before 1-1-2004 - GPF Account.
(ii)
For the employees who were recruited on or after 1-1-2004 - GPF-2004.
(iii)
For the employees of Autonomous Bodies / PSUs / Boards / Corporations
. etc. - GPF-SAB.
5.
Cash payment shall be admissible from 01-11-2024 i.e. salary for the month of
November, 2024 payable on 01-12-2024.
