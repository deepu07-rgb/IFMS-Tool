Sub: Grant of Dearness Allowance to State Government employees.
The Governor is pleased to order that the existing rate of Dearness
Allowance payable to the. State Government employees, drawing pay in the
Rajasthan Civil Services (Revised Pay) Rules, 2017, under Finance Department
Order of even number dated 15-07-2021 shall be revised from 28% to 31% with
effect from 01-07-2021.
2.
The term 'Pay' for the purpose of calculation of Dearness Allowance shall
be the Basic Pay i.e. pay drawn in the Pay Matrix of the prescribed Levels
and shall not include any other type(s) of pay like Special Payor Personal
Pay etc.
3.
The payment on account of Dearness Allowance involving fraction of 50
paisa and above may be rounded off to the next higher rupee and the
fraction of less than 50 paisa rnay be ignored.
4.
The amount of increase
in
Dearness Allowance for the period from
01-07-2021 to 30-09-2021 shall be credited to the GPF Account as per
provisions of Rajasthan Government Servants General Provident Fund Rules,
2021 as under:
(i)
For the employees recruited before 1-1-2004 - GPF Account.
(ii)
For the employees who were recruited on or after 1-1-2004 - GPF-
2004.
(iii)
For the employees of Autonomous Bodies / PSUs / Boards /
Corporations etc. - GPF-SAB.
5.
Cash payment shall be adrnissible from 01-10-2021 i.e. salary for the
month of October, 2021 payable on 01-11-2021.
