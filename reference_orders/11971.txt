ORDER 
Sub: Grant of Dearness Allowance to State Government employees. 
The Governor is pleased to order that the existing rate of Dearness Allowance 
payable to the State Government employees, drawing pay in the Rajasthan Civil 
Services (Revised Pay) Rules, 2017, under Finance Department Order of even 
number dated 28-09-2022 shall be revised from 38% to 42% with effect from 
01-01-20023. 
The term 'Pay' for the purpose of calculation of Dearness Allowance shall be the 
Basic Pay i.e. pay drawn in the Pay Matrix of the prescribed Levels and shall not 
include any other type(s) of pay like Special Pay or Personal Pay etc. 
2 
The payment on account of Dearness Allowance involving fraction of 50 paisa and 
above may be rounded off to the next higher rupee and the fraction of less than 
50 paisa may be ignored. 
3 
The amount of increase in Dearness Allowance for the period from 01-01-2023 to 
31-03-2023 shall be credited to the GPF Account as per provisions of Rajasthan 
Government Servants General Provident Fund Rules, 2021 as under 
4. 
(i) 
For the employees recruited before 1-1-2004- GPF Account 
For the employees who were recruited on or after 1-1-2004 GPF-2004. 
(i) For the employees of Autonomous Bodies / PSUs/ Boards/ Corporations 
etc. GPF-SAB. 
(i) 
Cash payment shall be admissible from 01-05-2023 ie. salary for the month of 
5 
April, 2023 payable on 01-05-2023. 
DA at revised rates as above would also be admissible to work charged 
employees as work-charged employees have been taken into their respective 
departmental service rules vide order no. F.14(4)FD/(Rules)/2006 Part dated 
13-03-2023.
