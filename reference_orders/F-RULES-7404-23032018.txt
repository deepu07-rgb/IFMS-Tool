GOVERNMENT OF RAJASTHAN 
FINANCE DEPARTMENT
No. F. 6(3) FD (Rules)/2017
ORDER
Sub: - 
Grant of Dearness Allowance to State Government employees.
The Governor is pleased to order that the existing rate of Dearness 
Allowance payable to the State Government employees, drawing pay in the 
Rajasthan Civil Services (Revised Pay) Rules, 
2017, 
under Finance 
Department Order of even number dated 09-12-2017 shall be revised from 5% 
to 7% with effect from 01-01-2018.
2. 
The term 'Pay' for the purpose of calculation of Dearness Allowance 
shall be the Basic Pay i.e. pay drawn in the Pay Matrix of the prescribed 
Levels and shall not include any other type(s) of pay like Special Pay or 
Personal Pay etc.
3. 
The payment on account of Dearness Allowance involving fraction of 50 
paisa and above may be rounded off to the next higher rupee and the 
fraction of less than 50 paisa may be ignored.
4. 
The amount of increase in Dearness Allowance for the period from 01- 
01-2018 to 28-02-2018 shall be credited to the General Provident Fund 
Account of the respective employees and cash payment shall be 
admissible from 01-03-2018 i.e. salary for the month of March, 2018 
payable on 01-04-2018.
5. 
The arrear of DA from 01-01-2018 to 28-02-2018 to the employees 
recruited to the Civil Services on or after 01-01-2004 and who are 
governed by Contributory Pension Scheme, shall be paid in cash.
6. 
The payment of arrears of Dearness. Allowance shall not be made 
before the date of disbursement of salary of March, 2018.
By order of the Governor,
