Printed-document recognition components

RapidOCR 1.4.4 and bundled PP-OCR inference models
https://github.com/RapidAI/RapidOCR
Declared distribution license: Apache-2.0.
Model files are supplied by the pinned rapidocr-onnxruntime distribution; their
bytes are included in the final portable SHA256 manifest. Models are evaluated
locally; employee documents are not sent to an OCR service.

ONNX Runtime 1.31.0: MIT. https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime
Headless OpenCV 5.0.0.93: Apache-2.0 plus included third-party component notices.
https://github.com/opencv/opencv-python
Shapely and GEOS, pyclipper, PyYAML and other transitive dependencies retain their
upstream notices. Distribution metadata and runtime license files are collected
in the portable package.

The headless distribution provides the same cv2 interfaces used for OCR without
GUI/video dependencies. Install the complete requirements.lock with --no-deps;
allowing RapidOCR to resolve its opencv-python dependency would install a second
OpenCV distribution and undermine the portable size budget.
