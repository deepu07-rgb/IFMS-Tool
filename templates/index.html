How to Use

Step 1: Upload IFMS PDFs - Inner Sheets, Schedule Sheets, or both
Step 2: Go to Employee Data tab to search, filter, and analyze salary data
Step 3: Go to Schedules tab for all 10 types: Income Tax, LIC, GPF, SI Premium, GPF 2004, ROP, Advance Salary, PPSS JDR, Coop Roadways, RGHS
Step 4: Use filters and export to Excel with one click

Tip: Upload all groups to get a complete cross-group searchable database!

Backup Safety Notes

Full backup includes the SQLite database, dictionary mappings, schedule component mappings, audit log, app settings, and backup manifest. Restore replaces the current database only after validation and automatic pre-restore backup creation. Backup actions do not include exports, uploads, generated Excel files, __pycache__, or temporary files. Use Download before moving backups outside this computer.

Help / User Guide

    
This app is designed to help increase office productivity. Human review of the data is compulsory before using the data.

    

1. Upload Inner Sheet PDFs from Upload PDFs.
2. Review Employee Data and open source rows when anything looks doubtful.
3. Apply Dictionary mappings only after checking raw designations.
4. Use Advanced Filters to isolate groups, months, pay scale, designation, and component issues.
5. Generate Derived Schedules from the Schedules tab and review reconciliation.
6. Compare Official Schedule PDFs from Official Schedule Comparison.
7. Run Monthly Comparison after at least two periods are available.
8. Build Master Register to track long-term employee identity and service history.
9. Create a full backup before major edits, imports, restore, or release use.
10. Use Export Center for review workbooks and final office exports.
    

    

      
Known Limitations

      
Human review is compulsory.
OCR is not included.
Official schedule comparison supports mapped layouts; unknown layouts go to review.
Monthly comparison is only as accurate as parsed Employee Data.
Master Register is an intelligence layer and does not replace official records.
The app is designed for local office productivity use.
